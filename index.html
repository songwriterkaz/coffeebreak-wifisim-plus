Let the other phone join

Type your hotspot name and password. They stay only on this phone. The other phone scans the code with its camera to join.

Your own wifi (home or hotspot)

Pair two phones on the same wifi by scanning QR codes. Text chat and voice calls then work with no internet.

It also works when one phone turns on its Personal Hotspot and the other phone joins it, with no internet needed.

Your own wifi limits: both phones must be on the same wifi and the router must let devices see each other (guest networks often block this). Some browsers hide local addresses, so pairing can fail on some phones. If it fails, use normal mode while online.
